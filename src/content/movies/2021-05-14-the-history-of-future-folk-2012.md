Definitely not what I was expecting - especially with the <i>incredibly</i> misleading poster that Netflix showed me - but it had its  moments of whimsical charm.

The music was the best part of this, which is good I suppose, given that it's one of the main focusses of the movie. Aside from that though, I felt like the storyline was pretty weak and it kind of fell weirdly somewhere between "indie musical" and "low budget comedy".
