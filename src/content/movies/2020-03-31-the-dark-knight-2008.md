This really is just <i>such </i>a good film.

It's no surprise to me that The Dark Knight still sits as the third highest rated film ever on IMDb. The 2 and a half hours positively fly by and, upon my most recent watching, I think I finally appreciate the storyline for how good it was, instead of just finding all the action sequences exciting.

The whole dynamic surrounding keeping Harvey Dent's image as that of an incorruptible, lawfully good man (and letting Batman take the fall for all of Harvey's shortcomings) was entirely lost on me up until now, and I'm so glad I took the time again to be able to experience it.
