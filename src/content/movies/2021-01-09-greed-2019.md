Tricky one, this...

I don't know whether I should be concerned by how well Coogan played the role of a mega-rich, super exploiting billionaire to be honest 😅

I definitely enjoyed this film; it was a fast-paced, punchy take on the modern day fast fashion industry shown through the actions of one selfish, hungry businessman. It's not hard to believe people like this exist in the real world. In fact, I'm pretty sure it's "based around" one Philip Green - give him a quick search!

However, Greed does fail to hit its mark a lot of the time. I think it would be a lot weaker without Coogan in the lead role, and I also feel some of the "social issues" brought up in the movie came across a bit last minute and tacked on (see: the docudrama-style end credits wall of text).
