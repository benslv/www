Not sure if the songs fully landed for me but there were definitely a couple that had me in awe at Tim Minchin's writing prowess. Like how do you fit every letter of the alphabet into a song so naturally and eloquently and cleverly?? So impressive!

Emma Thompson was a standout for me, too. I think she nailed her portrayal of Trunchbull in such a "love to hate her" way.
