This is a <i>great</i> sequel!

I really liked how this actually continued on the story from <b>The Shining</b>, and introduced its own, new ideas, without also being a re-hash of the previous movie (or adding new "lore" which doesn't already kind of work with the world set out).

It's been ages since I've seen Ewan McGregor act in anything, and I really missed him!
