Felt a lot slower and less intense than the first time I watched it, but perhaps that's just because I knew what to expect going into it.

This movie is disappointingly Generic Action Movie in my opinion, especially because I really like the concept behind Dredd and wish it would expand a bit more into Mega City One, instead of trapping us inside a big tower block and rehashing The Raid (2011).

Holding out for a sequel...maybe...not.
