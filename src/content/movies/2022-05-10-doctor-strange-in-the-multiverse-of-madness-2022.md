I <i>thoroughly</i> enjoyed how much of a Marvel tastebreaker this was.

Unsurprisingly, the story takes a bit of a back seat but Raimi more than makes up for it with his direction. You can tell how much fun he had playing around with the multiverse idea, and this movie was full of some really creative ideas and set pieces.

Easily better than <i>No Way Home</i> in every way and I shouldn't even have to argue my point.
