Just as good as the first one, I would say.

It definitely helped that I went to a back-to-back screening of the two movies (thank you Everyman for hosting it), and that <i>Part II</i> picks things up from pretty much exactly where the original left them.

Cooler setpieces, more characters, more story, more monsters! It's exactly what I'd hoped it would be.
