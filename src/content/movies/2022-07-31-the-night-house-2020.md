I definitely liked this! Was refreshing to watch something so <i>continuously</i> unsettling, instead of relying on a handful of "scary moments".

I felt like the 2nd act was a little too unclear for my liking, almost as if the movie itself was unsure where it wanted to go, but overall I thought it was very competent. 

There were some cool visuals (or lack thereof) that I thought were <i>really</i> clever, especially in their first few uses.
