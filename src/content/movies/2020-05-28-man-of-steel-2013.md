I now remember how much I hate it when the US Military gets shoehorned into films for no apparent reason.

<i>Man of Steel</i> was, for me, genuinely a really enjoyable superhero movie spliced with generic army content that shift the story from, "Alien being fights his own with massive, world-scale destruction," to, "Haha, US army goes brrrrrrrrr."

On the other hand, this film has Amy Adams <i>and</i> Henry Cavill in it...so maybe I can forgive its shortcomings 😍
