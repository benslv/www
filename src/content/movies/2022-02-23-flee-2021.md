It's very hard to know how to rate something like this. "Five stars," is my first instinct but I think it would be a disservice to reduce such an upsetting, emotional, inspiring piece of art to just a single number - at least not without talking more about why it deserves such praise.

I think a large reason I liked this so much was simply the huge disconnect and unfamiliarity with basically <i>all</i> of the subject matter and themes covered in the film. It sounds counterintuitive, I know, but somehow it helped me relate to - and understand - Amin so much more, fully removing myself from my shoes, and completely stepping into his.

I feel like I have a lot more I want to say about this film but I'm very much struggling to put it into words.
