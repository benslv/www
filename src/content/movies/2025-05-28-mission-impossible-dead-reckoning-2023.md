Definitely not as good as <i>Fallout</i> or <i>Ghost Protocol</i>, but that doesn't stop it from trying! Full of some incredible stunts and sequences, and I thought the 2h44m runtime absolutely <i>flew</i> by.

I think the story takes a major backseat in this movie, potentially to highlight the action even more, but I don't think that makes it any worse of a movie.
