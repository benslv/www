Still visually stunning and exactly what I want from a film adaptation of Dune. Casting is great and the sound design and soundtrack are so perfect too. The set design is so beautifully sci-fi yet still grounded in the medieval roots that run throughout the Dune franchise.

However...I felt all that the first time and didn't have any new thoughts about it upon rewatching, so I struggled to enjoy it to the same degree. I think maybe it's the lack of relatability to the characters that's the rub for me here; I'll never be a Duke's son forced to wander a desert and ally with its native people to avenge my fallen house.

I also got to see an early preview of a scene from <i>Part 2</i>, which was extremely exciting! My initial impression was that it had a very different tone to anything in the first movie, and I'm on the fence about whether that'll be a good or a bad thing. Guess I'll have to watch until March!

TL;DR: <i>Dune</i> looks really nice on IMAX.
