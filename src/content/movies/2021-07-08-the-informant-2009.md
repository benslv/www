An shockingly unbelievable story which grows and grows and spirals out of control as the movie progresses and the pieces of the puzzle fall into place.

Soderbergh perfectly combines the serious content matter of an investagtion into corporate fraud and embezzlement with the levity of an upbeat, almost slapstic sountrack to create a truly unique and enjoyable movie!

Matt Damon is, unsurprisngly, the linchpin of the entire film, carrying it from scene to scene with some really great acting and once again proving how talented he is at playing a huge range of different characters - I recently watched <i>The Talented Mr Ripley</i> and you'd almost think his parts were cast to two different people.

I really enjoyed this!
