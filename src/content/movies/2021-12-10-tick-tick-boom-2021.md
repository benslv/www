I'm not qualified to rate musicals because every song makes me well up with emotion and want to slap it with an instant five-star rating.

I've never seen any of Lin Manuel Miranda's work before - no, not even Hamilton! - but I definitely want to check out more of it now. 

Sadly I don't think I'll be finding any of the songs catchy enough to be singing them around the house, but they were good and I enjoyed listening to them. Garfield (not the cat) is absolutely incredible and he really does deserve the spotlight for his performance.
