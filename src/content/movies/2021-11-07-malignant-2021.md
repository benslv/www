What's up with the tone in this movie?

Is it trying to take itself seriously as a horror movie? Maybe, but then it goes and dispels all the tension it built up with weird cuts, strange choices of music, and questionable bits of dialogue.

Does it want to come across as a cheesy, goofy, slasher-esque flick? It could do? Why does it feel like it never fully commits to the part, then? 

Is that the point, that it's a mish-mash of both and kind of <i>supposed</i> to be all over the place and I'm just bad for not being able to appreciate it? Perhaps. That doesn't change the fact that I found it incredibly hard to get into, though. 

The third act was cool though, I'll give it that.
