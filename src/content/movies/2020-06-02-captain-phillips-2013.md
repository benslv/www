It's been 7 years since I first watched this, and I think it evoked an even stronger emotional response this time around.

The sheer tension, almost right from the get go, as you see events unfold both on and off the ship is exhilarating; I don't think I've watched many films that manage it so well.

Additionally, I'm so incredibly <i>grateful</i> that we're shown the pirates' sides of the story - it helps to blur the line between the 'good' and the 'bad' guys somewhat, as you begin to sympathise with Philips' captors.
