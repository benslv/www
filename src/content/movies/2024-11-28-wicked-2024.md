I can see why the 5-star rating people gave this 5 stars, I really can. The songs are so much fun, the sets are (mostly) gorgeous, and it really feels about as good as a film adaptation of a Broadway show could be.

But it's just not one of those 5-star movies for me. I still enjoyed it and will be going to see Part 2 when it comes out! :)

<i>Also</i>, Ariana Grande is really funny??? Her stage (screen?) presence is insane and you can tell she absolutely "gets" how to play <i>Guh</i>-linda to her fullest, especially in the first two acts.
