Wow, there's a lot that I loved here.

The absolute standout for me is Cliff Martinez's score, though. It's truly beautiful, and hauntingly melancholic in all the right ways.

Ultimately, I almost wish it was slightly longer? The ending felt quite rushed and I would've liked for more of the questions to be developed and resolved. Instead the movie seems satisfied with taking a slightly easier route out, and I think it was a missed opportunity.

I'll probably have the soundtrack on repeat all of tomorrow, though.
