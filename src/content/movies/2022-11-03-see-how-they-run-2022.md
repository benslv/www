A pretty solid, fun whodunit all-round.

With <i>Knives Out</i> arguably giving the murder mystery genre a bit of a renaissance back in 2019, it's hard <i>not</i> to compare it to <i>See How The Run</i>. There's no contest, really.

I never found myself invested in this movie's story, nor did I particularly care for any of its characters or outcome. At least in comparison to <i>Knives Out</i>, everything felt a bit underdeveloped and façade-like.
