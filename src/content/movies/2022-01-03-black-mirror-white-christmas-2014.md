This is the best one because Jon Hamm's in it.

Jokes aside it's an incredibly clever episode that keeps you watching every tiny detail,  with a lovely amount of "a-ha!" moments throughout to make you feel like a clever little audience member.

Definitely one of the best season of Black Mirror, back when it was still a refreshing, dystopian anthology series instead of the more recent, low-effort, "ooh look, technology is bad and scary" affair.
