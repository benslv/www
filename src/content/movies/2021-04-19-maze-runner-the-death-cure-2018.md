Honestly some of the most generic, uninspired drivel I've ever had the displeasure of watching.

<i>Death Cure</i> is all explosions, gunfire and shattered glass with no substance to back it up, coupled with every trope you could expect from a YA Sci-fi book turned movie.

And they didn't even have the decency to keep Newt alive to the end. 😡
