<i><b>Cameo: The Movie</b></i>

Not sure I loved this movie. The dialogue was very weird at times, almost as if the actors were just told to stand there and ad lib with each other. I also thought the sea of CGI was incredibly visible, and it pulled me out of the movie more times than I would've liked.

The third act also drags a lot, and continues to introduce more characters and plot points which really probably should've been wrapping up instead. 

I <i>did,</i> however, enjoy the movie's levity and the quick camerawork and fight choreography was really enjoyable to watch!
