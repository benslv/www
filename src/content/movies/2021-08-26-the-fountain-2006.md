Really wasn't sure how to rate this.

It's an ambitious storey which, I think, demands a lot from its viewers. Got a little convoluted at times but overall I enjoyed it! I quite liked the process of spotting the similarities on the three stories, and piecing together the information to "decide" what the symbolism was meant to be.

Glad it was a short movie, though, because it was a very long "short movie".
