I'm a huge fan of the original <i>Bourne</i> Trilogy, and I even enjoyed <i>Legacy </i>as well, but this one is far too bland and inconsequential to really be worthy of the <i>Bourne</i> moniker.

Far too much shaky cam, not enough downtime between the action sequences, and a laughably small amount of plot, all stretched to <i>two hours</i> of runtime 😵
