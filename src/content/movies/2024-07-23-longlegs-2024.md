Really loved what Perkins was going for in this movie but felt like it never escalated to the heights I was hoping for, and it ended more abruptly than I was expecting.

The tiny cast and often empty, deserted sets created a really unsettling sense of isolation and helplessness that I <i>loved</i>. Take the opening scene, with the sea of identical, new-build houses on an estate stretching to infinity, or her log cabin home hidden deep in the woods. Awesome stuff.

Nicholas Cage really pulled off something great with <i>Longlegs</i> the character, too. It's so close to silliness but ultimately a very creepy performance if you let yourself be scared by it.

Maybe it could've been a little gorier or had more jumpscares to be the horror movie I was looking forward to. I think about 90% of it was pure setup for a "finale" which didn't really resolve anything 😬

Oh I thought the lightning was <i>incredible</i> though.
