I felt a connection to this movie on a really compelling level.

This story taking place over the course of a single day in 90s New York, filled with a cast of eccentric caricatures and set to a wonderfully breezy yet emotional soundtrack make for an extremely authentic-feeling setting - one which I'd love nothing more than to be able to live in and experience for myself. 

The acting felt a little ropey at some points, especially near the beginning, but that to added to this film's charm for me. There were some lovely comparisons of similarity between different characters, too, which I would love to keep an eye out for again on another watch!

Schreiber absolutely stole every scene he was in, with the "educated, overly self-important writer" role he plays.
