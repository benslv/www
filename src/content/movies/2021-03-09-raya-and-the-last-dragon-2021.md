Easily the most visually impressive and stunningly animated movie I've ever seen. I really loved the first half's "episodic" style, and the soundtrack wasn't half bad either. 

<b>Raya</b>'s story isn't anything to write home about, though; it felt pretty cookie-cutter in its themes of loss, unification, trust, and humanity. However, that's almost to be expected from a Disney movie, and I went into it expecting as much, so I wasn't even disappointed.
