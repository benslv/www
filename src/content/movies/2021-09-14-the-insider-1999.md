I'm a stickler for dramas like this, so it's no surprise that I <i>really</i> enioyed this one.

Journalistic whistleblowing movies have been made so many times over and yet Mann manages to craft this one in such a delicate and realistic way, keeping it powerful and emphatic without feeling over-dramatised at any point.

The casting is superb and reminded me just how much I love watching Pacino in basically anything he's done; I really love him so much as an actor, even if he does kind of play the same character in lots of his movies 😝

Big praise for this, anyway. I was honestly contemplating giving it 5 stars because there's really so little I want to fault it for.
