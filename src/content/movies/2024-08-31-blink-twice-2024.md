Feel like more could've been done to develop the first third, either speeding it up or introducing more mystery, because it felt quite slow and repetitive.

Conversely, I actually found the jump to the reveal to be...too sudden? Like we're moseying along, piecing this puzzle together and than <i>bam</i> there it all is. And it's a depth of topic that I didn't really feel like the movie had earned. It's not a particularly serious movie and to—very quickly—try and wedge in something which <i>is</i> quite serious felt a bit wrong to me. Maybe it's the point that the (explained) motivations don't justify certain characters' actions, but I finding some way to add a few hints to that would've been cool.

Aside from that, because there are positives to talk about, I thought it was still a pretty good movie. Naomi Ackie was very strong in her lead role, and Channing Tatum very convincingly plays an evil tech bro.
