Really top-notch set design, props, costumes, CGI etc. Exactly how I like my sci-fi to look and I was loving it.

Not convinced so much by the story, though. Really felt like a string of video game levels, and some of these scenes even actually had the characters speaking as if they were giving a tutorial! Was hoping for more suspense and less action, but got it the other way round.

Cailee Spaeny though!
