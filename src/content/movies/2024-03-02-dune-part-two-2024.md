Everyone needs a hypeman like Stillgar.

It's very clear we're living through a special moment right now, where another great piece of literature has finally, properly been adapted for the screen, without compromising on runtime or sense of scale. The stars have aligned and Denis Villeneuve has been sent to guide us.

It's hard for me not to compare this movie to its older sibling, and I think this is where the rub lies with me at the moment. I really, really loved how precise and choreographed Part One was in its direction, and something about Part Two just felt rougher round the edges, and slightly more...detached? There are more character viewpoints to keep track of and it demands that the focus jumps around more to accommodate it.

I think I remember feeling similarly after reading the book, though, so maybe it's just a byproduct of having to adapt some much less straightforward material.

I still fully plan on seeing this a second time, which says a lot. I need another watch to fully appreciate the spectacle I just experienced.
