I never watched Whedon's Justice League so I suppose I have no point of comparison but I genuinely <i>really</i> enjoyed the Snyder Cut.

It actually felt like the movie used its four-hour runtime effectively, for the most part, and there weren't many points where I wish it had sped up or skipped over any scenes (a lot of those moments were concentrated nesrter the end, to be honest).

I think DC has managed to carve out a pretty decent spot from Marvel's (pretty sizeable) monopoly on the superhero movie market, setting itself apart as something a bit darker and more gritty; the fights are stronger, the lu ches hit harder, and I kind of felt like the characters <i>cared</i> a bit more, too. Maybe I've just been missing my fix of mainstream supe movies, though, so who knows?

If I had to pick out some things which I wasn't as much a fan of, I'd definitely go for the easy targets and complain about the over-use of slow-mo scenes (and also how <i>long</i> each one was!), as well as DC's inability to use any colours other than black, blue, and orange. I get that they're probably aiming for a consistent theme throughout they're movies but a bit more of the colour wheel would be greatly appreciated.
