Shame about the people across the aisle from me who decided the best use of their £16 ticket was to provide each other with a running commentary <i>all</i> the way through.

It's crazy how much respect <i>A Quiet Place</i> demands from its viewers, in terms of sitting as silently as possible, carefully eating their snacks to avoid crackling the packaging and incurring wrath of all the other moviegoers. And everyone complies!

Can't wait to watch the second part!
