What a strange movie!

I quite liked the change of pace and smaller scale of story that <i>Unsane</i> offered, and the somewhat "alternative" cinematography and framing definitely made for a novel watch.

Ultimately, however, I think the film was let down by an overlong, drawn out ending which seemed to contradict a lot of the themes being presented the first place, so it didn't fully work for me. 

Let's be honest, though, I only really watched it for the Damon cameo.
