It's now my headcannon that Ferris Bueller decided to go back into education as a teacher after discovering a newfound appreciation for the teachers he messed around back in 1986.

I had no expectations going into this film, but I'm very glad I watched it! the kind of stereotyped characters all work incredibly well together, and their stories are deftly woven into a main thread that stayed interesting and entertaining through to the very end.
