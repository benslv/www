<i>Foxcatcher</i> definitely starts slow, but the initial commitment to a bit of character development and exposition is entirely worth it for the tense, eerie atmosphere that shrouds the rest of the movie, the growing tension hinting at inevitable tipping point, a culmination of all the events that came before it.

There's lots to say about wealth and loneliness in this movie, as well as stark parallels that can be drawn between different characters and their desires for fame, stability, and recognition in the world.

I'm glad I ended up enjoying this movie! Loved Steve Carell in this, too, even though I suppose he did technically very little acting. Less is more!
