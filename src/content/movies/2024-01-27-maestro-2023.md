For the most part this just felt to me like a rushed, disjointed collection of events that occurred, rather than a cohesive story with a real point to make. Like why did this story need to be told?

That being said, Carey Mulligan absolutely crushed it and wholeheartedly deserves her Oscar nomination. This movie is about her and her alone.
