Really heartwarming, and a welcome change of pace from all the doom and gloom in the news at the moment.

It is depressing, however, to think/know that as soon as everything opens back up again life will go straight back to normal again and no-one will really bat an eyelid at the recoveries made by the planet and its species in our absence. Seeing how quickly the air pollution in some cities reduced once the roads emptied out was incredible; who'd have thought the Himalayas had been hidden behind that massive cloud of smog all this time!?

I hope enough people watch this because it was really good. Nature is healing and we are the virus.
