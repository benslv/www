Think the trailer was selling something very different to the movie. That's not to say I didn't enjoy the movie but it's always going to be a little disappointing when a set of expectations aren't met.

Anyway, sulk over. Lots to like here! The story is told in a particular way that really effectively builds the tension from multiple different angles at once. I actually cared about the characters and felt they were all given compelling motivations.

As with a lot of horror movies, it's at its best when we don't know what's going on and are only give the teeniest of peeks behind d the curtain. I don't think the ending fully landed for me but it was a very fun final few scenes! Are we allowed to laugh during horror movies? Genuinely funny in many places!
