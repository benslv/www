Serves as a stark reminder that revenge simply breeds more violence, anger and hatred, and is rarely (never) a satisfying solution to wrongdoing.

Bana is incredible in this. I haven't really been aware of him as an actor before but he portrayed his character very well, and  amazingly displayed his arc from almost vehemently believing he was doing the right thing, to massively regretting his actions and essentially succumbing to the paranoia of being on the receiving end of them.

I realise I haven't actually seen that many Spielberg movies, and I'm very glad I'm beginning to rack that tally up a bit.
