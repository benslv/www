One of the most beautifully-animated animes I've ever seen, rivalled only by <b>Your Name.</b>

Heartbreaking yet hopeful, this definitely worth watching, and it packs a surprising amount in its 45 minute runtime.

---

I read some other people's reviews and let myself be influenced by their opinions. It's definitely not as profound as it tries to be, and there are a few things that are quite difficult to look past when enjoying the film (namely a pretty sizeable, and questionable, age gap between the two characters).

Idk, I still enjoyed it but I completely see where everyone else is coming from.
