I think I liked it? It was genuinely hilarious and clever in places, with the theatre all erupting with laughter, but I feel like I didn't fully understand the message being conveyed?

I enjoyed the first act the most, when Nick Cage got to be a little sillier and goofier, because the rest was perhaps a little slow and wrapped up in its 😱 politics 😱

The cancel culture approach was pretty fresh and worked well with the subject material, though! There'll probably be a lot of people instantly decrying this as "woke" and refusing to ever watch it and I think they'll be missing out.

I will say, I thought the ending dragged a bit. There's a particular "dreamfluencer" advert I was expecting to see credits after...and then it just <i>kept going</i> for a while longer?
