Very much enjoying my foray into anime so far. Angel Beats was recommended to me by a friend, and what an amazing series!

I was a little put off by how surreal the first episode was (at least compared to <i>Erased</i>, which I'd just finished watching) but I leant into it and was rewarded with a funny, emotional, heartfelt show that left little else to be desired.
