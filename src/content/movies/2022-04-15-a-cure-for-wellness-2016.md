My oh my, what a goofy ahh movie.

The first act was really good, I'll say that. I <i>loved</i> the mystery shrouding everything, and I loved trying to figure out what was going on (because it was clear <b><i>something</i></b> dodgy was haopening) but after that, the movie was just a mess for me.

First and foremost, it's way too long. Could've easily been 2h and just as good, maybe even shorter. The feeds into my other main problem which is mainly just that Things are revealed far too disparately and at such a slow pace. The number of scenes where Lockhart learns a new Thing by walking into a secret room and slowly pacing around it...i don't have enough fingers.

And the ending. Oh, the ending. So out of pocket hahaha. Everything wraps up in like half an hour of the goofiest writing I've seen in a while, and it's super unsatisfying (but no less hilarious and amusing). 

I will say, though, there's one scene in the final third of the movie which made me feel really, like <i>incredibly</i>, ill. Points for that, I suppose. 

This movie is like Midsommar's older, but much less attractive, cousin.
