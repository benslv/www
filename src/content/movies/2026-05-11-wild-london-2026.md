Bees can get drunk on fermented nectar in the summer 🥹 and people are building “Hedgehog Highways” through their back gardens 🥹 and pigeons literally hop on and off the <i>tube</i> to get where they want to go 🥹 isn’t nature amazing???

Enjoyed this maybe even more than the “bigger” series like <i>Planet Earth</i> and <i>Blue Planet</i> because the intersection between humans and wildlife in these dense, urban spaces is just so, so interesting. Isn’t it incredible that we can be going about our days, living so close to all of these amazing ecosystems without even realising it?
