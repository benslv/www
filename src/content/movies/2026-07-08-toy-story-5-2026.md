Every new movie still manages to carry such an important message, and this is no exception. It felt a little on-the-nose at the beginning but I warmed up to it and ultimately the movie made a very balanced case for tech skepticism without outright rejection, which I think is a relatively healthy viewpoint. 

The writing is still just as tight as ever, and I don’t know how they keep managing to introduce new casts of toys that feel natural to the world and yet distinct in their own way.

Absolutely loved the Buzz Lightyear side-plot, too, and really enjoyed how it ended up connecting to the rest of the movie.

More of these please!
