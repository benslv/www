I just love, love, <i>love</i> Matt Damon so much! He was genuinely great in this role, and characterised the Midwestern, out-of-touch, American dad overcoming language and cultural barriers so well.

I was really surprised to see so many people online disliking this for bad acting and muddled story, because I picked up on neither of those. I thought <i>Stillwater</i> was a really well-paced, emotional movie with an incredibly compelling story.
