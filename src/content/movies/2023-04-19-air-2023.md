Sorry that 2h runtime absolutely <i>flew</i> by?? That's the sign of some great writing and acting, I guess.

Honestly such a funny movie! I was expecting something more serious in tone but very pleasantly surprised.

Matt Damon is and will always be a standout for me. He's great in everything and he's great in this. Affleck too. Ben Affleck is so good at these small bits of physical comedy. Like the way he sits and readjusts in a chair, or runs along a street, or enters a boardroom.

I wish there was more of this I could see.
