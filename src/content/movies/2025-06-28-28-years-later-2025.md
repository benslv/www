A fever dream of a movie in the best way. The choppy, frenetic editing and hyper-saturated look has only grown on me since I left the cinema.

Those same aspects definitely made it feel a bit rough around the edges at times, and I found myself wishing it could feel a bit more grounded in reality sometimes, only to promptly.change my mind and realise that, actually, this all makes it that much more interesting and fun to watch.

Very fleshed-out, lived in world. The set pieces and landscapes felt almost fantastical in a way, and I loved the occasional reminders that regular life was continuing on for everybody outside of the UK. They should probably quarantine us anyway, regardless of the zombies.

I didn't realise there was going to be a second (and even third!) part to this movie, so very excited to see that when it comes out!

Oh and it's hardly surprising but Ralph Fiennes stole the show for me. His stage presence is outstanding and he's a really great actor, is all I want to add.
