I fully expected this to be a low-effort, brash movie that one might find buried in the depths of Netflix under the "Laugh Out Loud Comedies" section. 

Instead I just spent the last two hours watching a beautifully heartwarming, thoughtfully-crafted, incredibly inventive story about, amongst other things, a man breaking free of his monotonous life through a seemingly innocuous interaction with a woman in a bakery. 

Will Ferrell gives the best performance of his career alongside Emma Thompson, Dustin Hoffman and Maggie Gyllenhaal, who all just light up this movie in the best of ways.

I'm so glad this subverted my expectations because that only made it all the more enioyable.
