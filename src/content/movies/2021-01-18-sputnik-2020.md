If you gave this to Hollywood, I honestly think they'd ruin it.

9/10 for most of it: the set design, the cinematography, the soundtrack, the acting. Really loved it!

I think it was just slightly let down by the story, as its pretty much a rehash of the "parasitic host takes over a person's body" trope.

Still very much worth a watch, though. Maybe you'll rate it higher!
