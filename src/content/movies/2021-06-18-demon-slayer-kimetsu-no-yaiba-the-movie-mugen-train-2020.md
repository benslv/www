Literal <b>chills.</b>

<i>Demon Slayer</i> is an incredible series in many ways, and <i>Mugen Train</i> serves as a great accompaniment to - and continuation of - the TV anime. 

In the space of just two hours we're treated to a great story, emotional character development, and some of the most wonderfully-crafted fight sequences I've ever seen, all coupled with the same amazing animation, sound design and soundtrack that made Season 1 so popular.

Highly recommend watching this if you've finished the first season of Demon Slayer, and I <b><i>massively</i></b> recommend watching the first season of Demon Slayer if you still haven't seen <i>that</i>!
