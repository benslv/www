It's a competent spy thriller, but it's also just a bit <i>dull</i>.

Carano really doesn't look like she's putting any effort into her acting whatsoever, and comes across incredibly wooden and uncaring for the duration of the movie.

The story is bland and uninteresting (and also a bit confusing), which is a shame because the cast has so much potential (McGregor <i>and</i> Fassbender!?) and I just feel like they're not utilised at all to do anything extraordinary!

The film is perforated with these long moments of silence, with or without dialogue, and I feel like Soderbergh was aiming to use these to inject tension and emotion into the scenes, when really all they achieve is a sense of awkwardness instead... 

The cinematography and soundtrack, on the other hand, are really quite good. David Holmes's score is jazzy, intriguing and mysterious, and Soderbergh frames his shots beautifully and really works to make every shot interesting. 

The fight scenes were also surprisingly good! They lack all of the intensity and punch of Hollywood action movies and are instead are full of long cuts and brutal choreography which serves really well in conveying their realism and keeping them feeling grounded.

Probably worth a watch, especially since its only an hour and a half long.
