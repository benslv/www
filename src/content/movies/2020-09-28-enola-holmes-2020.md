I realised about halfway through that I didn't actually care for the story that much.

I <i>really</i> didn't like the final 30 minutes or so.

The "breaking the 4th wall" stuff mostly worked quite well but I think there were a couple of moments where it fell flat or pulled me away too much from the film.

Unfortunately, I wasn't all that convincing as a Sherlock, unless he was aiming down  the more himbo Sherlock path.

Millie Bobby Brown was really good though, and carried the film for most of it, I'd say!
