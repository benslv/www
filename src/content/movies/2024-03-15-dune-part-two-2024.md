I guess all it took was a second watch, and I'm happy to say I'm so back on the Dune 2 hype train, baby. I'm a wormpilled, sandmaxxing Fremcel to the <i>core</i>.

Seriously though I think I was just stunned by my first watch because I enjoyed this one so much more and was able to devote so much more attention to the story and the acting, instead of just being bowled over by the incredible cinematography and set design. I saw Part 2 with Dolby Atmos audio this time, too, and what a difference to made. Night and day! It's so much easier to transport yourself to the surface of Arrakis when the wormsign actually, <i>literally</i> vibrates the room around you, and everything was just so much more immersive.

My initial concerns over Stilgar's character having been used for comedic relief were also quashed this time, which I was extremely relieved about. Bardem's incredible performance felt so nuanced and definitely leant a lot further towards the "crazed fanatic" side than it did for me before. This is a man so desperately blinded by faith that he'd find the signs in just about anything if it meant there'd be a Messiah for him to follow. In a way I think he acted really well as a representation of the Fremen people as a whole, especially by the end of the movie.

I'm really glad I went to see this a second time. I finally feel able to form an opinion on the movie because I could say after the first watch was, "I liked it," and any of my friends could tell you how unsure I came across when they asked me what I thought. This has definitely cemented <i>Dune: Part 2</i> as one of my favourite movies.

Also it's really embarrassing but there was a maybe 15-20 minute section in the middle of the film that I had absolutely no recollection of seeing before??? I'm not sure if I fell asleep or what but it was awesome to watch that for the first time!
