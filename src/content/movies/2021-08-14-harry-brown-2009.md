While it's hard to ignore the agenda that's definitely being pushed, this is a really good revenge movie. It's emotional, bittersweet, and elegantly crafted in the best of ways. Michael Caine is great in this, as well as all the other main and supporting characters on both sides of the story. 

As mentioned, though, it's hard to see past what essentially feels like a "good vs. bad" undertone stretching throughout the entire film. Clearly we're supposed to see the police as a force for good in the fight against London's estate-dwelling, violent, drug-riddled youth, but I think the dialogue around that is much more nuanced that is represented here. Definitely worth keeping this in mind when watching, because I caught myself more times than I expected actually rooting against the "heroes" of this story and siding with the "villains". If that's intentional then bravo, I think that's a good move.

Anyway, I was expecting a Kingsman movie, and I got something much better. I enjoyed this.
