Hmm, yeah... Interesting!

I'm not quite sure what my thoughts are on this one, because I didn't hate it but I also didn't like it all that much either.
It felt quite unsure in its footing, especially the first half, and was nowhere near as polished as I thought it would be.

Carey Mulligan is, unsurprisingly, amazing in her role and Burnham is...okay, I guess? I haven't seen him before in <i>anything</i> so this was his acting debut for me. Everyone else, however, felt like single-use characters, used solely for the purpose of moving a scene forward.

I think the storyline had a lot of potential and I was a little disappointed with how it ended, too. Would've liked to see it fleshed out more, although maybe that would be hard for an admittedly small-scale revenge flick like this - I think that's what frustrates me slightly: it could've been so much more, and had a much larger, emotional impact than it did.
