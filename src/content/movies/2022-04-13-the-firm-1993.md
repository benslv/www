Thus concludes the Cruise Cruise - three Tom Cruise movies in three days.

My takeaways from this epic excursion? Tom Cruise was in some great movies when he was younger, and he's a great actor! I feel bad that he's been typecast into such an action hero role nowadays because he has so much more potential range outside of, essentially, Mission Impossible.

This move itself was probably my least favourite of the trio I watched. I definitely felt the 2h34 runtime and think maybe half an hour could've been shaved off without anything too important being missed.

<i>The Firm</i> lacked intensity for me, I think. Nothing felt that high-stakes or tense, despite it being seemingly written that way. Actions had consequences, sure, but I didn't really care? And the events leading to the third act were just a bit dull.

But yeah, Tom Cruise was good. Maybe he should've taken one of those job offers on Wall Street instead after all.
