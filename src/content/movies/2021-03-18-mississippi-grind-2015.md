It took me <i>so</i> long to finish this movie. I started it three or four days ago, got distracted by something else halfway through, and then just couldn't find any reasons or motivation to finish watching it (other than so I could get it off my Plex and free up some space (1TB hard drive gang wya?)).

I didn't really enjoy <b>Mississippi Grind</b> as much as I was expecting. I'd seen people describing it as a "buddy film" and I guess that made me go into it expecting something a little more upbeat. Instead, I got a story about the depressing outcomes of a gambling addiction and a journey across midwestern(?) America - I know there's a lot more to analyse about this movie but I'm too pea-brained to do so myself. 

<i>Mendelsohn</i> and <i>Reynolds</i> are both very good in it, though, but I'm struggling to find much more to say really. I guess I just found it overall a bit of a...grind. *audience groans*
