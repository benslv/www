Honestly just pure vibes! This is the quintessential heist movie, with impeccable energy, a wonderful soundtrack, and a beautiful cast to boot.

It feels like the Soderbergh and the actors are just having so much fun with the movie, which really pays dividends for the viewer. The many locations are fabulous and grandiose (I now have a sudden urge to visit the French Riviera) and it's all so fun!!! 

I ate a <i>ton</i> of pistachios while watching this, not that it's relevant to either the review or the movie.
