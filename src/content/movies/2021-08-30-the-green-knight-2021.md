Yeahhhh this was right up my alley.

I think a lot of people have touched on how story-light this movie is, and I'm not going to disagree. The only thing I can say in rebuttal is, "yes but <b>holy shit it's so <i>beautiful!</i></b>".

Seriously, this is by far and away one of the most gorgeous, atmoshperhic movies I've ever seen. <i>The Green Knight</i> has it all: incredible cinematography, breathtaking scenery, lavish setpieces, a mysterious, captivating sountrack, and superb sound design to top it off. All the adjectives.

It's very A24 in all the best ways.
