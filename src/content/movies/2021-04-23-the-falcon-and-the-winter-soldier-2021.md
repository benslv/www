I didn't enjoy this as much as I was hoping. And I was <i>really</i> hoping it was going to be amazing.

When we watched the first episode all my friends bemoaned the fact it felt like we'd gone straight back to Phase One Marvel, with none of the crazy, wacky, magical multiverse stuff that WandaVision set up and basically worked off. Personally I wasn't too bothered by it and welcomed something a bit more "grounded in reality" instead of being too unbelievable. But boy oh boy, though, this shit got dull <i>fast</i>! I've become so used to Phase Three Marvel that watching a bunch of strong people punch each other and run around warehouses has become really dull!

I did like the political and humanitarian sides of this show, though, because I don't really feel like Marvel has taken the time to address them in anything else of theirs yet. Not sure I'm qualified to assess how <i>well</i> they covered them but I at least feel like it was taken seriously, sensitively and with the best of intentions?

Looking back I definitely enjoyed WandaVision more, which I'm surprising myself by saying, but FATW is definitely worth at least a 6/10 overall.

Praying for Loki to blow these previous two shows out of the water, ngl.
