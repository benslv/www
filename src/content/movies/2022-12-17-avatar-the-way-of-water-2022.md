Spectacular in almost every way. I'm actually speechless at how good the visuals were. Like, first-in-class good. Every other blockbuster released this year should be ashamed of their CGI. Is this what happens when you pay your artists properly? 

Obviously the story wasn't exactly strong, although I don't think anyone was really expecting it to be. It's complex enough to introduce new characters and world-building but that's about it.

My biggest pain point, to be honest, was the dialogue; did they write it all in one afternoon? Again, not a massive problem because it's not the "point" of the movie, but it would've been nice for them to talk like actual people, especially in the action scenes.

Oh, and you're seriously telling me they've had 13 years to work on this and nobody's found a better typeface to use for the exposition text than <i>Papyrus!?</i>
