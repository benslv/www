Really impressed with this film; I'm so glad I finally watched it!

It took me a while to realise but I think my favourite aspect was how each person embodied a different kind of "stereotype" and how their viewpoints were gradually changed by our non-Angry Man over the course of the film. 

Off the top of my head, I can remember:
<b>- Juror 1:
- Juror 2: </b>The Newbie
<b>- Juror 3: </b>The Stubborn One
<b>- Juror 4: </b>The Logical One
<b>- Juror 5: </b>The One-That-Can-Relate
<b>- Juror 6: </b>
<b>- Juror 7: </b>The Clown
<b>- Juror 8: </b>Our Hero 😍
<b>- Juror 9:</b>
<b>- Juror 10: </b>The Racist
<b>- Juror 11: </b>
<b>- Juror 12: </b>The Clueless One

OK looking back on that maybe they don't <i>all </i>quite fit into categories but I still think, beyond reasonable doubt (see what I did there 😉), it's one of the best parts of the film.

I didn't really believe I'd be capitavated by an hour and a half of pure dialogue but <i>boy </i>was I happy to be proven wrong. 100% you <i>have</i> to watch this as soon as you get the chance.
