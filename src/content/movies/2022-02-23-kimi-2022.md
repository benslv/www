Not sure it <i>quite</i> lived up to my expectations but I had a good time!

Kimi is essentially a pretty competent "Covid Zoom movie" cum action thriller which somehow manages to mention Covid in a way that feels completely natural and doesn't make the movie <i>about</i> Covid.

I didn't find the story particularly interesting, to be honest, and the soundtrack felt very slapped on at the last minute; there were multiple points in the movie where the soundtrack just <i>did not</i> mesh with what was happening on screen - and also one part where I really think it should've been complete silence instead.

Kravitz was really good, and I'm very jealous of her apartment. Wonder what the rent must be for a place like that.
