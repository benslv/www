I want to live in <i>Slow West</i>'s world. It feels like a simpler time; riding through massive, open plains, venturing through forests, trekking through narrow, towering canyon trails, with the only worries being the food in your bag and the people you travelled with. Occasionally, you might stumble across a lone trading outpost - or, if you're lucky, a small town - perfectly placed for you to rest, restock and continue on your journey, wherever that may be.

Yeah... I think that would be nice.
