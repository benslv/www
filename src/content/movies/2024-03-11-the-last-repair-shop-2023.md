I often forget that music is genuinely one of my greatest passions, and then I hear a story about how one man's $20 violin took him all over the world, or how a single mum's American Dream was realised through the opportunity to repair instruments for others, and it's just...waterworks 😭

When you think that we're really just a bunch of animals blowing air through metal tubes and banging on wooden boxes...for something so beautiful to come from that is completely and utterly incredible.

I should go and practise my saxophone.
