Jokes aside I'm <i>so</i> glad I decided to watch this for a third(!) time. I saw an Open Matte copy this time and had my eyes reopened at just how gorgeous every 👏 single 👏  frame of this movie is. The colours <i>popped</i>, the lighting <i>slayed</i> (slew?), and the cinematography and framing were just <i>perfect</i>. People often say that you can take almost any frame of this film and turn it into an A-Tier movie poster and they're goddamn <b><i>right</i></b>.

And the <i>sound design!</i> Such a wonderful, enthralling, rich atmosphere is created by Zimmer & Wallfisch's incredible score, combined with this incredibly detailed, lived-in sound design that brings every single set piece to life. The fact that I can sit there at the end of the movie and feel like I've <i>lived</i> in 2049's world is awesome.

I paid so much more attention to the story this time, too, where I'd previously just let the beautiful visuals wash over me. While it's definitely the weakest <i>part</i> of the movie for me, that doesn't mean it's <i>weak</i>. There's quite an expansive, thoughtful tale being told that starts simply and branches out into something more complex and rewarding by the end--and it gives the movie some great excuses to show off lots of different sets and sites in the process. It can be a bit slow at points but I almost wonder if the "aura" of the movie would be broken by trying to speed it up at all.

Anyway, yeah, bumping this up half a star because I really had such a great time re-re-watching this!
