Maybe I expected too much from a simple Disney animation but almost everything about this was completely unremarkable.

The story and writing were both incredibly cookie-cutter, filled to the brim with too many characters and not enough character development, and pumped full of clichéd lines and uninteresting dialogue. If you have even <i>half</i> a brain cell you'll be able to predict where the story's heading right from the get-go; I know this was written for kids but it's been proven time and time again that it's possible for movies like this to age well even when adults watch them.

The animation was great though, to be fair. I think it had a little bit of that "3D-but-2D" stuff that was used a lot in Futurama and it definitely added a lot of fluidity and depth to the movie, which is good given how much of it was set underwater!

Anyway, I don't know why I wrote such a serious review for such a non-serious movie. Treasure Island is better than this. Go watch that instead. Or maybe Sinbad. I remember Sinbad being amazing.
