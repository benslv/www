Watching this again really just reminded me how much I <i>love</i> this movie, and the fact that I found the same scenes equally as jaw-dropping and awe-inspiring as the first time I saw it is testament to the quality of the world Villeneuve has managed to create in his visualisation of <i>Dune</i>.

It's pure, clean science fiction—my favourite sort—with the added bonuses of great political drama and events transpiring on a galactic scale. The praise here is probably better attributed to Herbert's original novel but I think Villeneuve did an incredible job of translating it to film, at least compared to Lynch's attempt—although that's not a very high bar—and definitely having read the first book and knowing how everything plays out.

It's hard to deny that this is really just a setup film for <i>Dune: Part 2</i>, but I would wholly disagree with anybody who uses that argument as a reason for docking stars because I think this two and a half hour exhibition-fest was (a) extremely well-paced and meticulously crafted, and (b) entirely necessary for the next movie to carry any sort of weight whatsoever.

Definitely getting another 9/10 from me, and I'm really looking forward to the next one!
