What a strange thing for Marvel to just drop (I think) out of nowhere?? That's not to say I didn't enjoy it, though.

I really liked the first half, up to the end of the hunt, and thought the acting and cinematography were great! I don't know if they shot this on actual film but I spotted a few cigarette stubs on the screen at the end of some scenes, which was cute.

The charm basically vanished for me in the second half, though, where it felt like the production team just started trying to answer the question, "what if Marvel movie, but in black and white?" For me, this resulted in the feeling of something being missing (namely, any colour whatsoever) rather than the creation of an eerie atmosphere. I don't think Marvel action scenes work great on ye olde film.

Still thought it was pretty great, though! Giacchino crushed it with the soundtrack, too.
