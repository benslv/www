Watched this with my Dad instead of the new <i>Bob's Burgers</i> movie. Not quite sure how we progressed from one to the other...

We also both realised we'd seen it before. Probably together. Wonder what we decided not to watch in favour of this last time.

Anyway, I really enjoyed <i>Chronicle</i>. I love found-footage movies and thought it was pulled off extremely well in this. The CGI definitely looks a little dated, and it's possibly the only thing that draws me out of the immersion a little bit but it's not like you can do much about that the budget (which I think was quite low?)

Casting was great. Dame DeHaan is absolutely, <i>perfectly</i> placed in his role and I really enjoyed seeing Michael B. Jordan too.
