Whenever there's a story about Al Qaeda, terrorism, bin Laden, the American military, there's a risk that a movie will go full yee-haw gung-ho and glamorise the <i>crap</i> out it.

I so glad <b>Zero Dark Thirty</b> didn't.

As far as I could tell, <b>ZDT</b> was simply a gritty, on-the-nose retelling of events (from a US perspective, of course) following the hunt for Osama bin Laden and the people behind it. 

This is an epic tale spanning many years, and I really appreciated the division of the film into lots of little "acts", all of which culminate in the thirty minute, near silent finale which is an incredible payoff for an already incredible movie.

Chastain was perfect in her role, and I couldn't imagine many other people who could take her place as lead and display the same focus and determination she did. Honestly, and I know this is probably the point, everyone else was very incidental and only served as characters to play off and interact with hers. But I think that worked really well. 

100% must-watch if you haven't seen it already. It hits somewhat differently after having literally watched <b>The Report</b> the day before, too.

Not sure why I'm only giving it a 7/10, though. I think I'm going through a phase of doubting my own opinions.
