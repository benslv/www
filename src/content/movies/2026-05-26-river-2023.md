What a cute movie! Time loops can be tricky to do, given their repetitive nature, but I thought it was done really well here, with every “lap” feeling distinct and contributing to the overall story in a unique way.

Loved that there was a whole group of people trapped at the same time, because normally it’s just one or two people. Having an entire ensemble massively helps the story move forward, as everyone can be off doing their own thing, with brief check-ins every few laps. 

Fun watch, and I’ll be planning a trip to Kibune now 😁👍
