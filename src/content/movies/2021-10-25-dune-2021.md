How on earth did this actually meet my insanely sky-high expectations????? Denis I will forever be in your debt.

Honestly just incredible in every way I can think of:
- First of, it seems like a really faithful adaptation to me. I've read the first book and can't think of a single thing I would do differently in this <i>Part 1</i> movie. Dune is famously heralded as "un-adaptable" but I really think Villeneuve has achieved it. 
- The film is visually <b>stunning</b>. You know how everyone keeps saying, "find the biggest screen you can, and watch <i>Dune</i> on it"? Well they're 100% right. You just need to. It's amazing! It's massive, bold, colourful, awesome, and jaw-dropping in many, many ways.
- Hans Zimmer's score is potentially my favourite part. He's created such a rich sound filled with motifs that permeate every part of the movie, and it sounds so authentic and native to Arrakis, which I think is incredible. The sound design is also mindblowing, and I'm actually incredibly tempted to buy a ticket just so I can experience the Sardaukar planet scene again.
