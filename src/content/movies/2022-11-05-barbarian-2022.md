For my serious review, though, I first want to say that I was really, really prepared to rate this higher; consider this an extremely generous 7/10, mainly due to how much I <i>loved</i> the first half.

I thought <i>Barbarian</i> started incredibly strongly, with an intriguing premise and great acting by Campbell and Skarsgård. I was so very invested in the relationship between their characters, looking forward to how it would develop over the course of the movie. What a fool I was.

I can pinpoint the moments in this movie where I went from enjoying it to merely entertaining it, to basically suffering through it, which is a real shame. So many scenes in the final act felt cheap and rushed, as if the writers were approaching some hard-and-fast 1h40 runtime target and needed to wrap things up quickly.
