Pretty excited for this show! Will try and update with a score for each episode here.

<b>Episode 1:</b> 6/10
Pretty setup-heavy and felt quite like Phase 1 Marvel, which I liked. Introduced a bunch of characters early on and I'll be interested to see who sticks around for the whole series.

<b>Episode 2:</b> 5/10
Something definitely feels off about this series to me so far, but I can't quite place what it is. I think the story's getting off to a weak start, and I'm not sure where it's planning on going; it feels far too generic and not really linked enough to anything <i>that</i> "Marvel" at the moment. Glad to see the relationship between Bucky and Sam fleshed out a little bit and new Cap has the potential to become a more interesting character, I hope.

<b>Episode 3:</b> 7/10
Definitely feels like the story is coming together more now, or maybe I'm just finally understanding what's going on. Madripoor looks <i>very </i>cool, and I really hope we get to see more of it this series! Karli is still giving off a bit too much "angsty teenager" energy for my liking but hopefully that subsides a bit in the coming episodes because it makes it hard for me to care about her mission at the moment.

<b>Episode 4:</b> 8/10
Best episode so far! I'm writing this a good few days after I watched it, so my thoughts aren't that fresh, but some really interesting character arcs are beginning to form and I think that's going to make for a very good second half of the show.

<b>Episode 5:</b> 8/10
Damn lol, the Flag Smashers really aren't that scary a villain that have in a show like this. Ooh, spooky, they cause flash mobs of people and blow stuff up but are otherwise just small-to-medium-level terrorists? And their leader is just a teenage girl with Angst? Maybe that's the point? I don't know.
Glad to see Sam and Bucky acknowledge that they'll never be as close with each other as they were with Steve, and I think that was a big turning for them and for us seeing them in future shows. Also a massive "hell yeah" that Sam is picking up the mantle!!!

<b>Episode 6:</b> 6/10
Quite a disappointing finale to the series, although I don't really know what other direction it could've gone in. Very much hoping that's it for the Flag Smashers because, while their political motives are at least interesting, they're in no way cut out for being a full-scale Big Bad Evil (not that I think anyone's saying that).
