Bit disappointed that there weren't more Nonnas working in the restaurant. I had visions of Vince Vaughn captaining a legion of Nonnas, 5000 strong, all churning out cannoli and arancini at a pace never before seen by the average restaurant-goer. 

The fact that he only managed to enlist a paltry four Nonnas was probably the reason his restaurant was initially so unsuccessful. Had he simply increased his Nonna-count, I'm certain they would've seen more success early on.

This movie exists in the same cinematic universe as <i>The Intern</i>, where the elderly have been reintroduced to the workforce to compensate for the planet's diminishing and (ironically, ageing) population. The third and final movie in this trilogy could probably be something where pensioners are recruited into a construction firm to help build affordable housing for families.

Cute movie, though. Felt a bit obvious when it was trying to tug on the heartstrings, sometimes, but endearing nonetheless.
