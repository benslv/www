<b>Things I liked:</b>
- Glen Powell. This man is seriously charismatic and I totally agree that he's our best shot at a modern-day "movie star renaissance".
- The tornados. <i>Twisters</i> positions itself as a natural disaster monster movie and I think it works really well. The mythos and reverence surrounding storm chasing is pretty infectious and I found myself thinking I'd probably watch that YouTube channel in real life.

<b>Things I didn't like:</b>
- The pair behind us who kept talking to each other for the duration of the film.
- The three kids and dad to my left who held a running Q&A, at full volume, about who each character was and what their names were and why they were doing what they were doing.
- The family to my right (after we moved seats) who all repeatedly took turns, individually, walking past the screen to leave the theatre from about the halfway mark until the credits rolled.
- The mother who left her infant son alone in the cinema for the start of the movie and then returned, about 20 minutes in, with her phone flashlight on to search around for her seat and figure out where he was sitting.

Like I feel super grumpy about it because I thought the movie was really fun but honestly people need to <i>sit down</i> and <i>shut up </i>if they're sharing a cinema with other people who've paid to be there.
