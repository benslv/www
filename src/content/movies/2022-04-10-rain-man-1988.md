It's not often that I feel that burning, visceral <i>hatred</i> for a character in a movie, but Tom Cruise managed to evoke it within me. And that's great; I think it's a sign of some really good acting. 

Dustin Hoffman also deserved way more than an Oscar nomination for his role. I can't speak for how accurate his portrayal of someone with autism was but it definitely felt authentic and tastefully handled.

The soundtrack absolutely <i>slaps</i>, too.
