I find it so wild that this is <i>actually</i> a Predator movie. The disparity in technology between the Nomanche and the Predator is so large that it feels like the quality and believability of the movie should be seriously affected - in other words, "base Predator solos Native Americans, no diff" - but it actually works incredibly well.

Great levels of violence, beautiful scenery and set pieces, and the Predator is as cool as ever.
