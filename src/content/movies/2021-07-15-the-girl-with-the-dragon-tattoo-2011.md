Fincher, you amazing man, you've knocked it out of the park yet again.

Perhaps the fastest two-and-a-half hours I've ever spent, full of gritty cinematography, tense writing and an eerie, beautiful score to round things off.

Rooney Mara <i>absolutely</i> steals the show. This is her movie through and through, and it's made only better with the likes of Craig and Skarsgård performing their roles to perfection as well. 

The first half in particular is utterly incredible. It's fast-paced, intriguing and draws you in right from the start, until you reach the halfway mark and realise you're perching on the edge of your sofa, hand over your mouth, completely lost for words and desperate to know what happens next. 

I'm just sad there aren't more of these for me to watch. 

<i>Can we talk about that title sequence too? Amazing!</i>
