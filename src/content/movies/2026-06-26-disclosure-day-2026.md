Wasn’t everything I expected, but still a fun romp with some great acting! Loved that it wastes no time in explaining <i>how</i> Josh O’Connor stole those files—we don’t need to know!—and instead just drops us straight into the cat-and-mouse chase leading up to “Disclosure Day”. 

The trailer makes you think this is a movie about Josh O’Connor, but really it’s all Emily Blunt. She steals every scene she’s in, and genuinely played her character so well that I stopped seeming Emily Blunt at points. 

I was a little uncertain about what role Coleman Domingo really played in the movie? There was a lot left unexplained about how he knew what he knew and it sort of felt like his entire existence was to just lead our two protagonists to the finish line. 

Overall, though, an enjoyable movie that asks a very interesting question. I’m not quite sure I believe that society in real life would act the same way; all that footage would be brushed off as AI by anyone who <i>did</i> see it, and the rest of the world would’ve missed it while doom-scrolling ☺️
