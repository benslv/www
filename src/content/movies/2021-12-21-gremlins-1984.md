The scene in <i>Dorry's Tavern</i> is pure cinema gold, and I'm just astounded at the sheer chaos and variety in each shot.
The amount of effort it must've taken to coordinate the gremlins who, as I've been led to understand, were really difficult actors to work with on set really deserves praise in and of itself. 

I really enjoyed this, and it was a great way to feel some nostalgia and Christmas spirit both at the same time.
