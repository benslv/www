Wasn't what I was expecting, to be honest. The first two-thirds felt extremely angry and spiteful, which left a bad taste in my mouth, and the pacing was strange, rushing through lots of scenes and not really dedicating enough time to any one in particular.

Thomasin Mackenzie was really good, though, and I'm glad to see her in another big role!
