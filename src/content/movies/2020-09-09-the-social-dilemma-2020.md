Whilst I did <i>enjoy</i> this documentary, and agreed with the points it put forward, I can't help but notice the irony(?) surrounding the fact it argues against the "maximising engagement" model yet is a <i>Netflix Original.</i>

Seriously though, this is definitely worth a watch. Even if you're someone who thinks they're already aware of all the tactics social networking companies use to manipulate you and keep you glued to your phone it's refreshing to be reminded of such by a bunch of other people, all who at some point worked <i>for</i> those companies.
