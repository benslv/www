Forgot to log this at the time but I got to see a pre-screening of this and I wanted to brag.

Honestly I absolutely loved the first half of this movie. It felt suitably whimsical and magical, and the musical numbers were extremely catchy!

However, the second half bombed for me and I felt it fall heavily back into a generic, uninteresting, "defeat the bad guys" storyline just to tie up all the loose ends.
