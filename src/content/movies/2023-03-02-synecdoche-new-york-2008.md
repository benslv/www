I think it's important to sometimes challenge yourself with movies like this.

The last time I watched this, I was sitting in the kitchen of my first year university accommodation with my friend, watching it on the TV mounted on the wall at the end of the room.

We must've started it pretty late into the night because I remember us finishing it at maybe 1-2am and just sitting there like, "huh, okay..." a little bit lost for words while we tried to make sense of what we'd seen.

I say this now because that's exactly the same feeling I felt watching it a second time, tonight. This movie will forever be intrinsically tied to that specific memory, and I think that's pretty cool.
