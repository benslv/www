I was so sceptical starting this, sitting with my arms crossed, huffing because I wasn't allowed to pick a "proper" horror movie (I wanted to watch <i>Talk to Me</i>).

I was hooting and hollering by the end, though. This is the perfect sort of movie to watch with friends, shouting and laughing at the TV whenever someone makes a silly decision or dies a silly death, and I was fully ready to commit to the marathon and watch all three in a single sitting once the credits rolled.

Not a scary movie by any means but the lighting is great and it's fast-paced enough to make for easy watching. There's a particular scene with a bakery-related appliance that I just thought was <i>great</i>, too!
