This is a movie which thinks it's cool, and absolutely <i>is</i>. Such a funny script, Ryan Gosling is (as we already know) an incredible comedic actor, both physically and verbally; everything he does in this movie is absolute <b>gold</b>.

I really loved how self-referential and meta a lot of the scenes in this movie were and, combined with a staggering number of very cool stunts and practical gags, it really feels like the ode to stunt performers that I'm sure it wants to be—this is further reinforced with the behind the scenes shots during the credits, loved!

A final spattering of random stuff:
- Is this really Emily Blunt's normal accent??
- Aaron Taylor-Johnson doing a Matthew McConnaghey  impression very well
- You NEED....CARBS!!!
- Alien robot voice changers are so funny
- Waiting for a Director’s Cut with a full METALSTORM featurette

Very good movie. Very good fun. Go watch it if you haven't already.
