This film has <i>such</i> a strong first half but I feel like it's completely let down by a very weak and cheesy final act.

Levi is great as Shazam! and all of interactions between him and Freddie are a joy to watch. Just not too keen on the whole, "there's a whole family of Shazams now" stuff (even though I know it was in the comics).

Obviously very different to DC's Snyderverse, and I can't imagine they'd try and merge the two...that would be very strange ngl.
