I'm watching the Maze Runner trilogy because I'm desperate to know the story but don't fancy reading the books (and the Wikipedia synopsis is cheating). 

Fully expecting the following two movies to be utterly crap but this one really isn't too bad! As far as the storyline goes it's definitely interesting, and the cinematography and CGI are impressive enough too. I think the soundtrack could've been better, though, as it felt far too generic with a distinct lack of thought put in to creating a unique and appropriate atmosphere.

Overall, though, yeah. Not bad!
