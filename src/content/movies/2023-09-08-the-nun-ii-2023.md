OK I'll admit I enjoyed this more than I thought I would.

Visually quite appealing and I thought the sound design was good too!

I definitely felt painfully aware of the endlessly repeating cycle of "normal dialogue, creepy silence, expected jump-scare" about half an hour into the movie and it just continued like that for the rest of the runtime which was tiring to watch. Could've definitely switched up the pacing a bit  and maybe taken some time to build moments of proper tension.

I did enjoy how silly and aggressive the Nun was, though, and at least the violence and horror felt suitably crunchy.
