I’d watch this all again just for Act 3 (the first part). It managed to perfectly capture that sense of helpless, disinterested melancholy that seems to underpin so much of today’s world. The scene with Chiwetel Ejiofor and Matthew Lilliard was <i>so</i> good, and I would just love to see this somehow expanded upon even more. 

The rest of the movie was fine, but didn’t stand out to me quite as much. I struggled to connect with Hiddleston’s character as much as I would’ve liked, and Flanagan’s monologues still don’t really do it for me.

That being said, I think there’s a nice message being presented here, and it’s perhaps a comforting notion to think our experiences stay with us in even the most minuscule of ways, and shape who we are and who we become.
