Still a good movie but think it would’ve been better if I hadn’t read the book first. There’s just too much left out from the story that I think is important to know, and the movie felt reduced down to just the bare essentials to make it hold together.

Also, far too much music, and song choices that seemed quite disconnected from the movie’s overall vibe. It’s totally fine for some scenes to be in silence, and I think the directors should’ve leaned into that a bit more!
