I watched this as a three-episode supercut, and I still firmly believe this is one of the best adaptations of <i>A Christmas Carol </i>out there (although my only other reference point is the Jim Carrey version, so maybe worth taking my opinion with a grain of salt?)

Guy Pierce does an incredible job as Ebenezer Scrooge and I just love everything about this, <i>especially</i> the set design and soundtrack.
