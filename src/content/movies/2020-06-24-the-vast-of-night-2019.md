<i>"Charming"</i>

I think that's how I'd describe this film.

<i>The Vast of Night</i> was an intense, atmospheric film that told a beautiful story and made me feel an excitement and nostalgia for an era before I'd even been born.

So much is packed into its ninety minute timeframe that you'd expect the film to be fast-paced and energetic through and through; However, it's those moments when everything slows down - and to remember to breathe - that really made me fall in love with the unfolding story and its characters.

Visually, <i>The Vast of Night</i> is really quite a beautiful film. It has a lot of the 50s neon and Midwestern architecture you'd expect from such a film, and the camera work feels so superbly tied to the action and motion on screen that I couldn't help but be drawn in and fixated until the very end.

I didn't go into this film expecting to like it as much as I did, but I now <i>cannot</i> recommend it enough.
