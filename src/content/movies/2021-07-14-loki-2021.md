<b>Spoilers ahead!</b>

Mmm, yeah, well at least it was better than <i>Falcon & The Winter Soldier</i> (by a <i>lot</i>).

As with the previous two Marvel TV shows, I think Loki suffers from a case of Massively-failing-to-live-up-to-audiences-expectations Syndrome. The direction the showrunners took the storyline in from Episode 2 onwards was entirely not what I was hoping but you know what, I can live with that. 

They pulled my interest back with conspiracy theories and mysterious, unanswered questions, all hinting at some Grand Reveal™ in the season finale and guess what? <i>A fat load of Nothing!</i> No decisive end to the first season, only filler, exposition and setup to keep the viewers hanging on so Disney can milk their wallets just a little bit more with Loki Season 2, which <i>has</i> to be so much better, right guys? I'm sure everyone online will continue to rave over this show as if it's the second coming of Christ because Marvel have some dedicated fans yo!

...

Still miles better than <i>Falcon & The Winter Soldier</i>, though...although that's not a very high bar.
