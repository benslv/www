I was considering giving this 4 stars, but then I took a look at the other movies I'd recently given the same rating, and I read some other people's reviews, and I realised it definitely deserved higher.

<b>Sound of Metal</b> masterfully depicts the struggle of such a life-changing event, and how Riz Ahmed's character has to suffer, learn to cope with, and, in some ways, ultimately come to appreciate his new way of living life.

There were some truly crushing moments in this for me, and the entire film is supported by one hell of an incredible cast. I also want to applaud this film's sound design for so eloquently depicting what it would be like to be in Ruben's position. 

Probably my top watch of 2021 so far.

---

Also, I watched the first half of this movie without subtitles, before thinking that it might possibly be a good idea to turn them on in case it shed any light on the sign language used throughout. In my opinion, it didn't really add anything, so if you can do without then you won't miss out.
