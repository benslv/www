Really wasn't a fan of this show to be honest.

I don't know if I just missed out on what everyone else loved about it, or if it was an <i>Emperor's New Clothes</i> situation but I just feel like there was so much build up in the first few episodes...and for what? Basically nothing of any note. 

The finale was a massive let down and I honestly believe you could skip the entire show and not miss out when you come to watch any of the Phase Four Marvel movies. I don't think that's a great sign for the quality of a TV series.

p.s. Don't even get me started on the episode lengths and the amount of credits in each. Maybe I would've enjoyed it more if I'd waited and binged it all in one go.
