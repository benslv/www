Boy oh boy, this <i>really</i> grew on me as I kept watching. The retelling of events from each perspective was handled very well, with each one adding an extra layer to the - already uncertain - truth, constantly challenging the viewer to pick a side and person to believe.

The duel at the end was simply incredible; I haven't seen a fight on-screen like that in while, and the intensity bled right through the screen (no pun intended) to fill the room I was in.

I do feel the ending could've been a little better wrapped up, since the "truth" is left rather ambiguous, but, perhaps that's the point? I doubt any single retelling covers all the facts with perfect accuracy 👀
