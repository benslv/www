I was fully expecting a low-effort, cheesy video game movie but I actually really enjoyed this!

Most, if not all of the jokes and gags landed perfectly for me and the attention detail in how players interact with video games worlds was super appreciated - I even noticed the classic "player struggling with poor internet and lagging everywhere" character in the background for a couple of scenes, which was hilarious!

Definitely petered out towards the end as it felt they were trying to shoehorn in a weak romance subplot but overall I had a blast.

Oh yeah also <i><b>Jodie Comer</b></i> 😍
