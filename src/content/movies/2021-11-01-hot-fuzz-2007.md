<i>"It's alright Andy, it's just bolognese!"</i>

Honestly amazing how crazily quotable this movie is. I must be on my third rewatch by now at least and I still have such a blast mouthing along with the dialogue and reciting all the little quips scattered here and there throughout.

The amount of care and attention that's been put into every scene and piece of dialogue to foreshadow the later parts of the story and link all the little bits together is astonishing, and it's incredibly satisfying to pick up on more and more things with every watch.

Definitely my favourite in the Cornetto Trilogy without a doubt.
