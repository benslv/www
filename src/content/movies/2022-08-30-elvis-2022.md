Thoroughly, thoroughly disliked most of this movie. Especially the editing, and <i>especially</i> the editing in the first half. 

Almost all of the film felt like that fast-paced, flashy, narrated exposition that's supposed to take up the first twenty minutes before settling down into calmer, more dialogue-focused scenes with longer shots and more thoughtful acting.

But I know Baz wasn't aiming for that.
