Hailee Steinfeld carries this movie, undoubtedly. Matt Damon is okay, and Jeff Bridges is fine. They've both played better roles than this.

Considered turning on the subtitles more than once because some of the lines in this film are delivered with such a southern drawl that they might as well be speaking gibberish.

Also there's a guy who gets about 5 minutes' screen time near the end where he just makes horse and pig noise, and it's just <i>never</i> addressed afterwards???
