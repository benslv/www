I'm really sorry but I actually quite enjoyed this movie!

It's a Hollywood cheese-fest turned up to 11 but it does it <i>so</i> well and feels <i>so</i> gratifying to watch.

Aliens that shoot <i>spikes</i> from 5-meter long <i>tentacles</i> on their <i>backs</i>? Mental. 

Swarms of said aliens climbing up the sides of buildings? Mental.

Hand-to-hand <i>combat</i> with the Alien Queen on the edge of an arctic precipice? Fucking <i><b>mental.</b></i>

I had a <i>lot</i> of fun and I'm not ashamed to say it.
