I don't really watch movies while I'm at uni and I honestly felt a little "out of practice" just sitting myself down and <i>properly</i> watching something again.

Within about 15 minutes I was already hooked and reminded of all the reasons I love movies: watching a carefully-crafted story unfold, looking out for and picking up on all the tiny, little details put in by the director to add depth to the screen and, in the case of <i>The Guilty</i>, experiencing the edge-of-your-seat tension from I right through from start to finish.

This was a really great movie to start my Christmas holidays with. I enjoyed its relatively small-scale environment - I'm a sucker for "it all takes place in one room" movies - I loved the atmosphere built up through the dialogue and sound design, and it had an incredibly satisfying third act which I just think was really, really good.
