Maybe it's just because I've never played a proper Dungeons & Dragons campaign but I feel like I just spent the last two hours of my life watching all of the most popular fantasy franchises smushed together into an overly-sanitized, satirical, characterless "Best Bits" compilation video. 

That's not necessarily a comment on the quality of the film <i>itself</i>, though, which was...fine? It was visually great and competent in all the ways I was expecting but I never felt particularly engaged or invested, which is frustrating because I'd hoped I would enjoy it as much as all the reviews I've read.
