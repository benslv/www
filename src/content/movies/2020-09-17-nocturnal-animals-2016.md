Definitely deserves more than a 7/10, I think, but I didn't give it the attention it deserved this time around. 

This is one of those films that I really need to watch a second time and/or discuss with someone else in oder to feel like I "understand" it. Its pretty straightforward in premise - there are two interleaving stories being shown throughout the film - but it definitely went over my head a bit in how (and, to be honest, <i>whether</i>) they link together and play off each other.

But yeah, a solid watch, good cast, amazing scenery and atmosphere.
