Very sad about this, because Apocalypse is a <i>Cool Character</i>, but this is a <i>Bad Movie.</i>

It felt just very flat all the way through, with no actual emotion or reason for me to care about any of the story or characters. Frankly, I was more interested in rooting for Oscar Isaac to take over Earth instead of the X-Men stopping him, which says a lot, I think.

Glad I watched it, but only because I'm trying to watch all the X-Men films, and this was the second to last one standing in my way. 

Also, it's <b>two and a half hours</b> long. 😠
