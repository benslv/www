I feel like I should've enjoyed this more than I did.

Helloby is a really likeable character, but there was just no reason for me to feel invested in anything that happened!
I think the villains could've be improved a lot, as a clockwork Nazi that spins its swords around lots just doesn't really cut it (choosing to ignore Rasputin and his 101 Hellhounds because they really are just inconsequential to the entire storyline).
