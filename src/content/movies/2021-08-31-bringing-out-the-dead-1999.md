Some very powerful, moving stuff.

I didnt really enjoy it as much as I thought I would - felt like there was a lot of "dead air" at time, pardon the pun - but, then again, it's not the sort of movie that lends itself to being <i>enjoyed</i>, per se.

Nicholas Cage definitely gives one of his best, most nuanced performances I've seen, as this exhausted paramedic working a never ending, thankless job.

Definitely worth a watch.
