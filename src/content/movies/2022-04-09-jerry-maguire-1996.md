I really enjoyed this! While technically a rom-com (I think), I didn't find it too soppy or cheesy, and the humour was a perfect balance between blink-and-you-miss-it background gags and wonderfully dry, clever quips that catch you off-guard and land really well; Laurel's entire character feels constructed around this and she's <i>great</i>.

Tom Cruise was as Cruisey as ever, which I personally don't mind. I enjoyed his acting and the role suited him well. His relationship with Rod had such a satisfying journey, and made for a perfect conclusion to the story.

I wish it was a little shorter, though. Two and a bit hours was stretching it a bit, and you could definitely feel the length (uwu)
