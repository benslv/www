<i>Prometheus</i> kind of reminds me of the <i>Cloverfield</i> "trilogy" in the sense that it really feels like this could've been a standalone film at first, with the Alien parts tacked on at the last minute to tie it to something more substantial and perhaps help with ticket sales on release day. If it weren't directed by Ridley Scott himself then I think I'd really be entertaining that train of thought.

Visually, I really enjoyed this. It's modern enough to have great CGI and combines that with sweeping vistas and some nice practical effects to create a movie which feels like a realistic and upgraded version of the orignal <i>Alien</i>.

The story, on the other hand, is pretty dire. It's hard to believe that a company spending $1 <i>trillion</i> would hire a crew of such incompetent people and feel comfortable sending <i>them</i> off to be the first people to meet humanity's creators. Because of this, it's no fun watching all the terrible things that happen to them because...you can completely see it coming? It would be much cooler to watch an actual, <i>functioning</i> team of people do everything right and <i>still</i> just get absolutely wrecked by the monstrosities created by those Engineers.

There are also far too many questions left unanswered (some of which I'm not sure were done intentionally) with the only solace being that a <i>few</i> of them are answered a little bit more in the <i>Alien: Covenant</i> (or as I like to call it, <i>Alien: Convent</i> - how cool would it be to have a movie with a bunch of Xenomorphs running around dressed like nuns??)
