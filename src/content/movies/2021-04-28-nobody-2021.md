Glad I watched it, and the first half or so felt very novel and entertaining!

Was let down a little bit in the second half with the classic "evil Russians" cliche and the budding up with old friends trope, which just led to it all feeling a bit hollow for me.

Odenkirk is absolutely great, though, and I really need to see him in more stuff! Might even have to put myself through Breaking Bad so I can then watch Better Call Saul or something.
