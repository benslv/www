Hmm, what a strange little movie!

I decided to watch this before <i>Thunder Road</i> mainly because it was the one I had immediately on-hand so definitely an interesting introduction to Jim Cummings. Would be interested to see if he acts like this in his other "big movie" too.

Tonally, this was all over the place, and I can't tell how I feel about it. I don't think I <i>dislike</i> it but it definitely made it hard to get into. I was expecting a mysterious, suspenseful, thriller about a cop in a small town solving a mystery (à la <i>Fargo</i>, <i>Twin Peaks</i> or <i>Wind River</i>) and, uh, <i>The Wolf of Snow Hollow</i> was resoundingly Not That.

Didn't hate it though. Definitely something you just have to watch and decide for yourself about.
