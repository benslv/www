Just gotta get through that first act, with all its exposition and flashbacks and "previously on" explainers because, once you're through, there's an extremely fun action movie waiting on the other side, full of amazing stunt set pieces and quintessential Mission Impossible drama and peril.

If I had to criticise one thing...if I <i>had</i> to...it would be that perhaps there were one too many doohickeys, thingamajigs, and whatchamacallits that needed to be connected, synced, and hooked together before they could defeat The Entity. Imagine if they'd forgotten their USB adapter!
