<i>"the only way to assert our right to publish, is to publish"

I enjoyed this! It was well-paced, had a stellar cast - the kind where you keep pointing out, saying, "oh! He/She was in [show]!" all excitedly - and fitting soundtrack too.

The only slight downside was that it was fine. That's it. Just fine. I felt it was lacking something. Some oomph. Not sure why though, but it's why I don't feel able to give it above a 7.

Still very enjoyable, though, and a fine addition to the collection of "press discovering massive scandal" genre.
