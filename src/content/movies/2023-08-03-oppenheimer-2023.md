Oppenheimer is a massive movie that demands a lot from its audience and I just kept thinking to myself in the cinema, while watching this beautiful movie, how lucky I was to be able to experience it in IMAX as God...I mean, Nolan, intended.

Basically everything about the movie is perfect, I'd say. The 3 hour runtime is long, and I definitely felt it, but it's worth it for such an awesome crescendo of events, all branching off and building on each other, ultimately leading up to that moment with the final test.

Speaking of, I don't think I've ever heard a cinema so quiet before; you could've heard a pin drop in the front row. Everyone was just completely transfixed on picture in front of them, waiting for the impending shockwave to blow us out of our seats. You know, I hate to say it, but I actually almost felt like I was there <i>with</i> the characters, witnessing the event firsthand.

Everyone brought their A-game to this film, and I really, <i>really</i> hope Emily Blunt gets the recognition she deserves; maybe a Best Supporting Actress? Matt Damon is Matt Damon and I think he needs a mention for often bringing a bit of levity to such a heavy film; he's always good in his roles and I love the man. Ironically I don't have many strong feelings about Cillian Murphy's performance, one way or another!

Good lord I want to fucking <i>sob</i> at how emphatic and powerful Göransson's soundtrack is, too. He already has such a stacked list of movies in his portfolio and this is just yet another, perfectly capturing the essence of the movie and translating it into a sound that fills every corner of the room and plays with emotions I didn't even know I had!
