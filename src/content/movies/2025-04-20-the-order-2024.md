Wow. Really surprised at how good this is, and how little I'd heard of it.

No doubt this is a pretty bleak movie. The tone feels so subdued, almost hopeless, the entire time, with the ending—rightfully, in my opinion—really never landing on any sense of "good triumphing over evil".

There are no winners in a fight like this. Everyone just ends up worse off than before.
