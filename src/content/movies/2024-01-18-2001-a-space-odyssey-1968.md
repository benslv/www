OK I'll be really honest I don't see what's so special about the first half of this movie. I completely forgot how little dialogue there is and, at the risk of being ridiculed as someone who can't appreciate "real cinema", I'll admit I actually almost nodded off a few times...although that might've just been because the ambient space sounds were super relaxing and I haven't been sleeping enough lately.

We had a brief intermission halfway through and then, all of a sudden, I felt so invested and interested in the story. There's no doubt that the HAL chapter and the psychedelic finale are the strongest sections of the film, both linked by their explorations of an intelligence which surpasses that of humankind, one seeking to destroy, and the other seeking to improve and evolve.

And past that, I don't really know how much merit there is in trying to analyse this film. I don't think there's an adequate way to combine everything together in a way that doesn't leave <i>something</i> else out. You just have to sit there with everything and be content that it makes a really good movie.
