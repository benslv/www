It feels kinda weird to see Wolverine in a movie with any reference to X-Men whatsoever, but I don't think it was bad by any stretch.

The action sequences were really fun (especially the bullet train) and I appreciate how Wolverine's abilities are beginning to be framed more as a curse of life now. It sets up the theme for movies like <i>Logan </i>really well. 

I do think it was probably a little long for my liking, and the final act was definitely quite anticlimactic but it overall it was a good watch and a neat addition to the X-Men series (even though it basically has very little bearing on any of the other movies)!
