I feel very calm after watching Ad Astra. That's pretty much all I can say. I'm gonna try and actually write some kind of review (or my thoughts, at least) because I honestly feel the film deserves it. 

Ad Astra did an amazing job of mirroring the calm, measured approach taken by the main character (Roy McBride) in most situations and I was pleasantly surprised by it, having gone into the film expecting an Interstellar-style romp across the solar system with the usual hurdles to overcome and plot devices to explore. Every scene had an air of tranquility to it, which I think worked wonders to reinforce Roy's personality upon us: unphased, calm and collected.

Visually, Ad Astra was <i>stunning</i>. I think this might be my favourite space film if I had to rank them on wow-factor alone. I loved the futuristic yet realistic aesthetic of the spacecraft, stations and other technologies, as well as the beautiful planet shots as the film moved forwards.

For some reason, this film really struck a chord with me and I felt compelled to write some stuff about it. Definitely recommend watching it if you haven't.
