If you want to go fast, go alone. If you want to go far, go together. The Barkley Marathon is both.

Putting aside the obviously inhuman fitness that's demanded of these human runners, their support team also plays such a factor in getting them over the finish line. Found myself tearing up at seeing how devoted Gary's friends and family were to helping him achieve this goal. His victory is their victory.
