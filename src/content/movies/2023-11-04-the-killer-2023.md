Had an absolute blast watching this, and loved basically every second.

Fassbender plays the anonymous hitman with such a satisfying level of neurotic care and precision, and it's hard to imagine anyone else being quite as good in the role.

I really loved the fight choreography in that one particular scene midway through; the shaky cam was maybe a <i>bit</i> too shaky for me but the energy was there and every punch carried great sense of weight behind it.

Not sure how much I'd get out of a rewatch, to be honest, but I'll almost certainly stick it on once it's streaming on Netflix. Super glad I went to see it in the cinema first, though!

Also Fincher includes the word "normie" in this so canonically he's a 4chan user in my head now.
